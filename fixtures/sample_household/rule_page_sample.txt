Medicare levy for individuals

The Medicare levy helps to fund the sample health system. Most people who live in the sample
country pay it with their income tax.

How much is the levy?
The Medicare levy rate for 2026-27 is 2 per cent of taxable income.

Low income reduction
A lower levy applies if your income is low, and the levy phases in above the threshold.
For 2026-27 the Medicare levy low income reduction single lower threshold is $2,801,100.
For 2026-27 the Medicare levy low income reduction family lower threshold is $48,000.
The Medicare levy low income reduction family threshold increase for each dependent child is $6,500 for 2026-27.
The Medicare levy low income reduction family reduction rate is proposed to be 8 per cent from 1 July 2026, subject to legislation.
