# Recent changes

When you add or correct a Valuation, Finance360 tells you what it moved. A short message appears at the bottom of the screen: "You added a Valuation for Everyday. Net position moved by $1,200.00 (up)." It also says when a question became more ready, for example "What do we own and owe? went from Partly ready to Ready." Use **Take a look** to open the screen that changed.

## Where the messages are kept
The message disappears after a few seconds, but it is kept as a line in **Recent changes**: a section of [Home](/) below Your questions (the last ten you have not dismissed), and the [Recent changes](/recent-changes) page, which lists every one, with the ones you dismissed shown muted. The **x** on a line dismisses it. Each is kept for 30 days and then removed. Recent changes is in your backups like everything else.

## When you get one
Only when the write really moved something: the net Position changed by at least one whole dollar, or one of your questions changed how ready it is. Saving the same figure again, or a change of a few cents, says nothing. A Valuation dated in the past that does not change today's Position adds no net line.

## What it never contains
Only the Account's name and an amount, in words. Never an account number, a provider's reference or anything from a document.

## Coming later
Removing a Valuation, committing an Import and saving a Super statement will say what they moved too.
