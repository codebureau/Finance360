# Keep track of tax

For when you want to know how this financial year's tax is shaping up, and keep the papers behind it. This is one common way to start; see [Start here](/guide).

## What to provide
- The Household and its Members, once. Every Tax record belongs to a Member.
- Tax records as the year goes: income, deductions, and Documents (a receipt or statement) for each.
- After 30 June, one income statement per employer for each Member.

## Screens that matter
- [Tax records](/tax/records): where everything goes in.
- [Income reconciliation](/tax/reconciliation): where your pay went, from the income statements.
- [Tax estimate](/tax): the tax for a financial year, worked out from your Tax records.
- [Tax what-ifs](/tax/whatifs): try a change on a year without touching your records. Optional.
- [Saved estimates](/tax/estimates): estimates kept exactly as calculated, and a comparison of any two of the same year. Optional.
- Read [Tax](/guide/tax) first.

## Ignore for now
Accounts, Imports, Instruments, FX rates, Reference data, Income streams and the Projection. The Position and Projection will look empty, and that is fine.

## Growing into another way of using it
Add Accounts to start keeping track of investments, or add Accounts, Income streams and Assumptions to build the whole picture. Your Tax records stay exactly as they are. See [Keep track of investments](/guide/path-investments) and [Build the whole picture](/guide/path-whole-picture).
