# What to provide, when

A schedule of what Finance360 needs from you, and when. Each item says which starting point it suits, for example **tax**, **investments** or **whole picture** (the whole picture uses everything). Frequencies are a sensible rhythm, not a rule. [What's due](/) lists what has gone stale; the lines marked "Shown in What's due" below are the ones it watches, and how often it asks depends on your Effort level.

## What the home screen is
[What's due](/) is the home screen. It is calculated from what you have entered, so it can never disagree with your data. It asks only about what you already use: with no Tax records, for example, it never mentions tax. It says what Finance360 is currently using and what refreshing would improve, and never calls anything overdue or late. Snooze (one week, one month, or skip this period) hides an item for a while and you can bring it back from the Snoozed section. If a check cannot run, What's due says "Could not check" rather than hiding it.

## Once, then when it changes
- **Household and Members** (tax, investments, whole picture). Names and dates of birth, on [Household](/household). Everything else hangs off a Member.
- **Household facts** (whole picture). Couple or single, homeowner or not, and which Property Account is the principal home, on [Household](/household). Once, and again only if your situation changes. A blank one uses a labelled default, so you only need to enter what differs, and you will want to choose the principal home if you own more than one property. They feed the Age Pension calculation in the projection.
- **Accounts** (investments, whole picture). Add each one on [Accounts](/accounts). Close one when it ends; Accounts are never deleted.
- **Income streams** (whole picture). A salary for each Member on [Income streams](/income-streams). Change it when pay changes. You type take-home pay yourself, unless a Scenario switches on Tax in the projection, which estimates it instead (see [Projection](/guide/projection)). The [Tax estimate](/tax) also uses a salary stream to fill a Member's salary for a year in which you have entered no Tax records for it (see [Tax](/guide/tax)).
- **Assumptions** (whole picture). Returns, inflation, retirement ages and spending, on the [Projection](/projection). The Best, Likely and Worst Scenarios start with placeholder figures, so review them before you trust a result.

## Automatically, every day
- **Prices and FX rates** (investments, whole picture). Fetched each evening (18:30 Household time unless changed), but only if a provider has been set up for your install. The [Reference data](/reference-data) screen tells you: it shows the last success and any error, or "Not set up" if there is no provider. If it says that, enter Prices on the Instrument's page and FX rates on [FX rates](/fx-rates) by hand. An Instrument with no Yahoo identifier, such as an unlisted fund, is never fetched and always needs hand-entered Prices.

## Valuations for Accounts you value by hand
How often depends on your Effort level (Light, Steady or Thorough). Add each Valuation on the Account's page, from the statement or your own estimate. The Position keeps using the last figure on or before the date, so an old one quietly makes the total less accurate; What's due tells you which.
- **Valuations for cash and loan Accounts** (investments, whole picture). A loan's Valuation is the amount owed, as a positive number. Light: every 3 months · Steady: monthly · Thorough: monthly. Shown in [What's due](/).
- **Valuations for super Accounts** (investments, whole picture). Super funds often send statements only half-yearly. Light: every 6 months · Steady: monthly · Thorough: monthly. Shown in [What's due](/).
- **Valuations for property and vehicle Accounts** (investments, whole picture). An estimate with a note of where it came from is fine. Light: not asked · Steady: yearly · Thorough: yearly. Shown in [What's due](/).

## Yearly, after the fund issues it
- **A Super statement for each super Account** (investments, whole picture). Once a year, usually August to September, type the fund's annual statement on the super Account's page: the balance roll, and the breakdown, contributions received and Investment options if you want them. It also records the 30 June Valuation. Attach the fund's statement PDF under Documents on the statement or the Account. See [Accounts and imports](/guide/accounts-and-imports). Enter the **concessional contributions received** and **Division 293 released** lines if the statement shows them: the Tax estimate uses them for [Division 293](/guide/division-293) (until you do, it counts the employer super on your Income statements). Not shown in What's due yet.

## On each export
- **Investment platform files** (investments, whole picture). Add the trade file and the cash file on [Imports](/imports), review what is staged, then commit. A later export that overlaps an earlier one replaces it for the dates it covers, and the earlier rows stay retrievable.

## When you first hold an Instrument, then when its mix changes
- **An exposure profile for each Instrument you hold** (investments, whole picture). Open the Instrument's page, choose Enter exposure profile, and type the fund's published split from its factsheet in each Dimension it publishes (asset class, region, sector, exposure currency; leave one blank if the fund does not publish it), with the date it is as at and where it came from. Once when you first hold it, then again when the fund publishes a new mix (a quarterly or yearly update is plenty). Weights within one point of 100% are accepted as printed. For a fund that holds other funds, also add each underlying fund and its share (and give each of them a profile): typed by hand for now; reading them from the fund's documents is **coming later**. Without one, the Holding appears as "No profile" in its Account's look-through, and nothing else is affected. Not listed in What's due yet; a reminder for a stale profile is **coming later**. See [Accounts and imports](/guide/accounts-and-imports).

## As it happens
- **Tax records and their Documents** (tax, whole picture). Add a record when you pay a deductible cost or receive income, and add the receipt under Documents on the record's page. See [Tax](/guide/tax) and [Library](/guide/library).
- **Depreciating assets** (tax). When you buy something you will use for work or a business over several years, add it as a Tax record of type depreciating asset: cost, start date and effective life. It adds a deduction to its Return label each year without you re-entering it. If you tick *claim in full*, check the asset is eligible first. See [Tax](/guide/tax).
- **Home office hours** (tax). If you work from home, add one Tax record of type home office hours for each financial year: either a regular arrangement (your hours per day and the weekdays you are at home, or the office days a month, with a table you adjust for leave) or casual hours (the hours you actually worked each month). Fill it in after each month, or once at the end of the year. See [Tax](/guide/tax).
- **Car kilometres** (tax). If you use your own car for work, add one Tax record of type car kilometres for each financial year and list your work trips in it (the km of one trip and how many times you made it). Add trips as you go, or all at once at the end of the year. See [Tax](/guide/tax).
- **Tax what-ifs and saved estimates** (tax, optional). Nothing is required of you. When you are weighing a purchase or a change in pay, add a [Tax what-if](/tax/whatifs) for the year to see its effect without touching your records, and press Save estimate on the [Tax estimate](/tax) when you want to keep an answer to compare with later. See [Tax](/guide/tax).

## After 30 June (mid-July)
- **An income statement for each employer, for each Member** (tax, whole picture). Enter it on [Income reconciliation](/tax/reconciliation) or [Tax records](/tax/records), or print the ATO page to PDF and read it into the form (see [Tax](/guide/tax)). Use it instead of plain salary and tax-withheld lines, never as well as them. Light: from 15 July · Steady: from 15 July · Thorough: from 15 July. Shown in [What's due](/).

## On the notice of assessment
- **The lodged result, for each Member** (tax, whole picture). Enter the amounts from the notice on the [Return worksheet](/tax/lodged) to see how close the estimate came, and add the notice under Documents there. When the tax office amends an assessment, enter the amended notice: the earlier one is kept. Optional: nothing is due until you want the comparison. Shown on no [What's due](/) list. See [Tax](/guide/tax).

## Yearly, for the whole install
- **Rule figures for the new financial year** (tax, whole picture). The Rule pack holds the tax tables and the yearly rates (car kilometres, home office), and the [Tax estimate](/tax) will not calculate a year it has no figures for. Open [Rule figures](/rule-figures), choose the year, and capture each figure that says Missing (usually in July), or press **Start from last year** to begin with last year's figures and then check each against the official page: those stay **provisional**, and the estimate says so, until you confirm them. Nothing needs a release. See [Rule figures: keeping official rates current](/guide/rule-figures).
- **A Rule figure for each year your records need** (tax, whole picture). When a Tax record reads an official figure for a financial year that nobody has captured yet, What's due says so, one item for each figure and year, and links to the [Rule figures](/rule-figures) screen with only the missing figures showing. Until it is captured the record adds nothing to any total. Light: straight away · Steady: straight away · Thorough: straight away. Shown in [What's due](/).
- **A captured Rule figure that a release now ships differently** (tax, whole picture). If you captured a figure and a newer release ships a different value for the same year, your figure stays in use and What's due shows both. **Keep mine** records that you have seen it: it is not raised again unless a later release ships yet another value. **Use shipped** withdraws your captured figure (the Audit trail keeps it). Light: straight away · Steady: straight away · Thorough: straight away. Shown in [What's due](/).
- **A captured Rule figure that no longer fits** (tax, whole picture). A release can change what a figure may be, so one you captured no longer passes the check. It is ignored (the release's figure, if there is one, is used) until you capture it again on [Rule figures](/rule-figures). Light: straight away · Steady: straight away · Thorough: straight away. Shown in [What's due](/).
- **A provisional Rule figure that is now firm** (tax, whole picture). You captured a figure as announced but not yet law, and a release now ships the settled figure. The release's figure is used. **Withdraw mine** tidies your captured one away. Light: not asked · Steady: straight away · Thorough: straight away. Shown in [What's due](/).
- **A Backup check** (all). The sidebar footer says when the last Backup ran, in a warning colour if it failed or is stale.
- **The restore exercise** (all). Once a year, restore a Backup somewhere safe to prove it works. The steps are in `docs/restore-runbook.md`.

## The reference and system screens
These are the last group in the sidebar. You will rarely need to open them to add data.
- [Instruments](/instruments): things held in units and priced, such as ETFs. You add one when you first hold it, and an Import tells you when one is missing.
- [Rule figures](/rule-figures): the official rates and thresholds for each financial year, shipped with the release or captured here. See [Rule figures: keeping official rates current](/guide/rule-figures).
- [FX rates](/fx-rates) and [Reference data](/reference-data): foreign currency rates, and a check on the daily fetch of Prices and FX rates.
- [Categories](/categories): the list of Account types, read-only.
- [Audit trail](/audit): every change and sign-in.
- [Settings](/settings): density, your sign-in and security.
