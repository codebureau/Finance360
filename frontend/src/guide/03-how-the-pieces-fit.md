# How the pieces fit

Left to right: what you provide, what Finance360 works out from it, and the question it answers. Each row is one chain.

:::pieces

## How the areas link up
- **Members come first.** Every Account, Income stream and Tax record belongs to a Member (a Tax record can also belong to no one, for a household fact). Add them on [Household](/household).
- **Accounts make the Position.** The [Position](/position) is the Accounts added up by Member and Category. Its figures are also where the [Projection](/projection) starts.
- **Exposure profiles show what the Position is made of.** A fund's profile (its split by asset class, region, sector and exposure currency) turns an investment Account's value into a look-through, and the [Exposure](/exposure) screen adds those up for the whole Household, by Member, Category and Account. Accounts with no look-through (cash, property, loans) are listed as Not analysed, so the total is still the Position. See [Exposure](/guide/exposure).
- **Imports, Prices and FX rates make an investment platform Account's value.** The Import brings in the buys, sells and cash. Prices say what a unit was worth on a date, and FX rates convert a foreign currency to AUD.
- **Income streams and Assumptions drive the Projection.** A salary pays take-home pay into Cash until the Member retires. You type that take-home figure in, unless a Scenario estimates it.
- **Tax records make Label totals, and Label totals make the Tax estimate.** The [Income reconciliation](/tax/reconciliation) reads the income statements among your Tax records.
- **The Rule pack holds the dated figures** that tax and the Projection both rely on, such as tax tables for each financial year, super rates and ages.
- **Tax can feed the Projection.** A Scenario can switch it on to estimate take-home pay (see [Projection](/guide/projection)). Otherwise you type take-home pay into an [Income stream](/income-streams).

- **Home is the starting point.** [Home](/) shows the net Position, then **Your questions** (how ready each answer is, worked out from the same Accounts, Valuations, Members and Tax records, with the next step to improve it), then What's due, which says which figures could be refreshed.

- **Ask sits above all of it.** The **Ask** button, on every screen, searches this Guide for the best passages to your question. It reads no Account, Valuation or Tax record and changes nothing. See [Ask a question](/guide/ask).

## What sits underneath
- The [Audit trail](/audit) records every change.
- Backups, once set up, run each night and cover the database and every document in the [Library](/library).
