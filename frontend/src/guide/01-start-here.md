# Start here: why are you here?

Finance360 keeps one household's money in one place and answers a few plain questions from it. You do not have to use all of it. Pick the starting point that matches why you opened it today. You can change your mind later: nothing is hidden and nothing needs a restart.

Most people start with one of these. They are examples, not a complete list, and using Finance360 some other way is just as fine.

## Common starting points
- [Keep track of tax](/guide/path-tax): Tax records, their Documents and income statements, and the tax for a financial year.
- [Keep track of investments](/guide/path-investments): Accounts, Valuations and Imports, and the Position.
- [Build the whole picture](/guide/path-whole-picture): everything, with near-term and long-term linked up.

Each page says what to provide, which screens matter, what to ignore for now, and how to grow into another way of using Finance360.

## How hands-on do you want to be?
This is a different question from the starting point. It sets how much Finance360 asks you to refresh, and there is no better or worse answer. You choose it in the What's due section of [Home](/), the first screen, and you can change it at any time.
- **Light** asks only for the few things that move your numbers most, a few minutes every few months. Your figures are roughly right. This is where everyone starts.
- **Steady** adds the slower-moving items, a few minutes a month. Your figures are more accurate.
- **Thorough** asks for everything Finance360 can use as often as it helps. Your figures are the most accurate.

The level changes how often Finance360 asks, never what you can use. Nothing is switched off at any level.

## Not sure what a word means?
Words with a capital letter (Position, Account, Member, Tax record) are the names Finance360 uses everywhere, and the Guide uses them the same way.

## A starting point is not a setting
It is only the screens you choose to look at. Nothing is switched off, so you can step into another starting point, or back out of one, whenever you like.
