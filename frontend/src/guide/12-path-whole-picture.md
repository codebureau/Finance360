# Build the whole picture

For when you want near-term and long-term to link up: what you have, what you earn, the tax on it, and whether it lasts. This is one common way to start; see [Start here](/guide).

## What to provide
Everything in [Keep track of tax](/guide/path-tax) and [Keep track of investments](/guide/path-investments), plus:
- Income streams, once, then when pay changes.
- Assumptions (returns, inflation, retirement ages, spending), once, then when your thinking changes.

## Screens that matter
All of them. A good order is [Household](/household), [Accounts](/accounts), [Income streams](/income-streams), then [Position](/position), then [Projection](/projection), with [Tax](/guide/tax) alongside.

## Ignore for now
Nothing, but it is fine to build it in stages: Position first, then Projection, then Tax.
