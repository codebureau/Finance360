# Keep track of investments

For when you want to see what you own, and how it has moved. This is one common way to start; see [Start here](/guide).

## What to provide
- The Household and its Members, once.
- Each Account, once, then a new Valuation when its figure changes (monthly is plenty for cash, super and loans).
- For investment platform Accounts, the exported files. Finance360 works out the value from them.
- An exposure profile for each Instrument you hold (its split by asset class, region, sector and exposure currency, from the fund's factsheet), once, to see what an Account is made of. For a super fund Account, map each Investment option on its Super statement to a fund first, then give that fund a profile.

## Screens that matter
- [Accounts](/accounts) and [Imports](/imports): where figures go in.
- [Position](/position): what you own and owe, today and over time.
- [Instruments](/instruments): open one to enter its exposure profile. An investment Account's page then has its look-through, which you can switch between asset class, region, sector and exposure currency.
- [Reference data](/reference-data): whether Prices and FX rates are arriving.
- A fund of funds: list its underlying funds in its profile (typed by hand for now; reading them from documents is **coming later**). The look-through marks what came through them. See [Accounts and imports](/guide/accounts-and-imports).
- Read [Position](/guide/position) and [Accounts and imports](/guide/accounts-and-imports) (which also explains exposure profiles).

## Ignore for now
Tax, Income streams, the Projection and Assumptions.

## Growing into another way of using it
Add Tax records to keep track of tax. Add Income streams and look at the Projection for the whole picture. Your Accounts and Valuations carry straight over. See [Keep track of tax](/guide/path-tax) and [Build the whole picture](/guide/path-whole-picture).
