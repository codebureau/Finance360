# Position

The [Position](/position) answers "where do we stand?": everything the household owns and owes, on one screen, as at a date. It is built from your Accounts, so it is only as current as the Valuations and Imports behind them.

## What you see
Pictures first, figures one click behind them.
- **Net position**: one big figure, with Assets and Liabilities beside it and a sentence on what to notice ("Net position is ...; Property is the largest share of assets"). Liabilities appear here and are never drawn as a slice of assets.
- **What it is made of**: how your assets divide, by Category. Each Category keeps its own colour on every screen (Cash is teal, Property is terracotta, and so on), and a legend beside the chart lists every part with its share and its exact figure. Use **Category / Member** to see the same picture by who holds the assets instead.
  - **Chart type**: the three small icons switch the picture between a donut (the default), a stacked bar and bars. Your choice is remembered for this card, on every device you sign in on.
  - **Use as my default**: appears when this card's picture differs from your default. Press it to make that picture the starting point for any other chart of the same kind (more charts gain the switch later, coming later). Switching on its own never changes your default.
  - **Other**: at most six parts are drawn. If you have more Categories, the smallest are drawn together as "Other" in the picture; the legend still names each one underneath it, and the figures keep every line.
  - A picture that cannot be a donut (a negative part, or nothing to divide) is drawn as bars with no switch.
- **Over time**: a chart of Assets, Liabilities and Net at each month-end (Net is the bold line in plain ink; Assets and Liabilities each keep one colour, with Liabilities dashed; each line is named at its end and in the legend, and hovering a month shows all three figures; a hollow marker is an incomplete month), with a sentence on how Net position has moved. It is open when you arrive. **Show figures** gives the same numbers as a table.
- **Show all accounts** at the foot: the tree of Class (Assets and Liabilities), then Category, then Account, with every figure. Each Account links to its own page. With Layout set to Table, the figures open by themselves as a table.
- **Change view**: one row holds As at, View, Tag and Layout (collapsed; it shows what is chosen).
  - **As at** a date. Leave it as today, or pick a past date to see the Position then.
  - **View**: Household (the combined figures), By Member (a column for each Member, plus Joint) or Joint (only jointly held Accounts).
  - **Tag**: narrow to Accounts carrying a Tag you made up, such as "emergency fund".
  - **Layout**: Tree or Table for the figures.
- Inside the figures, a line shows how the net figure adds up: each Member's solely held Accounts, plus Joint, equals the Household.

## What it is made of, underneath
The [Exposure](/exposure) screen splits the same Position by what the investments hold (asset class, region and so on), for the Household, each Member, Category or Account. See [Exposure](/guide/exposure).

## Where each figure comes from
- A manually valued Account (cash, super, loans, property, vehicles) shows its latest Valuation on or before the date. A super fund Account's page can also split that value by what its Investment options hold; see [Accounts and imports](/guide/accounts-and-imports).
- An investment platform Account shows a value worked out from its Holdings and the Prices on that date. See [Accounts and imports](/guide/accounts-and-imports).
- Liabilities, such as a home loan, are listed under their own heading and taken off to give the net figure.
- A foreign currency Account is converted to AUD with the latest FX rate on or before the date.
- A closed Account counts only for the dates it was open.

## When the numbers look wrong
- **"N Accounts are not in these totals."** Each of those has no Valuation, or no FX rate, on or before the date. Open them from the tree under Show all accounts and add the missing figure.
- **A total looks too low or too high.** Open the Account and check the date of its latest Valuation. An old one is used as it stands.
- **A past date looks incomplete.** That is expected if an Account had no Valuation yet. The chart draws such a month as a hollow, "incomplete" point.

## Ignore at first
Change view (Tags, the By Member and Joint views, the table layout) and the chart type. The first two cards and the Household view are enough.
