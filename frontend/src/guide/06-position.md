# Position

The [Position](/position) answers "where do we stand?": everything the household owns and owes, on one screen, as at a date. It is built from your Accounts, so it is only as current as the Valuations and Imports behind them.

## What you see
- A totals bar, then a tree of Class (Assets and Liabilities), then Category, then Account. Each Account links to its own page. Switch to a table with the Layout choice.
- **As at** a date. Leave it as today, or pick a past date to see the Position then.
- **View**: Household (the combined figures), By Member (a column for each Member, plus Joint) or Joint (only jointly held Accounts).
- **Tag**: narrow to Accounts carrying a Tag you made up, such as "emergency fund".
- Underneath, a History section (closed until you open it): a chart of the net figure at each month-end, with a table of the same numbers.
- A line showing how the net figure adds up: each Member's solely held Accounts, plus Joint, equals the Household.

## Where each figure comes from
- A manually valued Account (cash, super, loans, property, vehicles) shows its latest Valuation on or before the date.
- An investment platform Account shows a value worked out from its Holdings and the Prices on that date. See [Accounts and imports](/guide/accounts-and-imports).
- Liabilities, such as a home loan, are listed under their own heading and taken off to give the net figure.
- A foreign currency Account is converted to AUD with the latest FX rate on or before the date.
- A closed Account counts only for the dates it was open.

## When the numbers look wrong
- **"N Accounts are not in these totals."** Each of those has no Valuation, or no FX rate, on or before the date. Open them from the tree and add the missing figure.
- **A total looks too low or too high.** Open the Account and check the date of its latest Valuation. An old one is used as it stands.
- **A past date looks incomplete.** That is expected if an Account had no Valuation yet. The chart draws such a month as a hollow, "incomplete" point.

## Ignore at first
Tags, the By Member and Joint views and the table layout. Household view and the tree are enough.
